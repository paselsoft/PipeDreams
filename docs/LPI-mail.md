## Testo da inviare (inglese)

Dear LPI Learning Materials team,

My name is [Nome Cognome], and I am an Italian Linux enthusiast. I develop **Pipe Dreams**, a
free browser game that teaches Unix pipelines (grep, sort, uniq, cut, tr, sed, xargs, redirections
and more). Every pipeline in the game is verified against real bash and GNU coreutils, and the
game shows the equivalent shell command so that players can try it on a real Linux system.

- Game: https://paselsoft.github.io/PipeDreams/
- Source code: https://github.com/paselsoft/PipeDreams

The game is free, has no ads, no accounts and no commercial purpose.

I have just added a section that helps players prepare for **LPIC-1 (101 and 102)** and
**Linux Essentials**. For each exam objective it shows the Italian title, the weight, the
commands from the official objectives, and a **link** to the corresponding Italian lesson on
learning.lpi.org. It also shows which levels of the game practise that objective. The section
does not include any text from your lessons. It credits the Linux Professional Institute and the
volunteer translators, and links the CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 license.

I would like to ask two things:

1. **Offline lessons.** Would you agree to let me include, inside the game, the **complete and
   unmodified** Italian text of a few lessons that are closest to what the game teaches: 103.1,
   103.2, 103.4, 103.7 (exam 101) and 105.2 (exam 102)? They would be shown exactly as published,
   with the title, the version, a link to the original page, the full attribution and the
   license, so that players can read them offline next to the exercises. I understand that the
   CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 license already allows sharing unmodified copies for non-commercial
   purposes, but I prefer to ask you first and follow any guidelines you have.
2. **Attribution.** Is there a preferred way to credit LPI and the Italian translators (for
   example, specific names or a standard sentence)?

If it is useful, I would be happy to know whether a project like this could be listed among the
free resources of the LPI Publishing Partner program.

Thank you for the excellent free materials, and for the work of the Italian translators.

Kind regards,
[Nome Cognome]
https://github.com/paselsoft
